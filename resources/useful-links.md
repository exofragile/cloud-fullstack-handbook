# 🔗 Useful links

Every link from the original PDF's "Useful Links" section, organised by chapter, with a short note on what each is good for. 🧭

> **How to choose:** **W3Schools** pages are short, with try-it-yourself editors (great for a first look). **MDN** pages are deeper and more precise (great when you want the real details). Use both.
